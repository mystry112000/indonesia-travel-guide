Best Time to Visit

Month-by-month Indonesia travel guide

Planning • 8 min read

Indonesia is a year-round destination, but timing can make or break your trip. The archipelago spans the equator, so it's tropical — hot and humid year-round — with two distinct seasons: dry (April to October) and wet (November to March).

The best time to visit most of Indonesia is April, May, June, September, and October — the shoulder months when the weather is good but crowds are thin.

By Island

  • Bali: Best Apr-Oct. Jul-Aug peak. Nov-Mar rain but still sunny mornings.
  • Java: May-Sep for Bromo and Ijen. Oct-Apr clouds reduce volcano views.
  • Sumatra: Apr-Sep for jungle trekking. Oct-Mar for surf (Mentawai).
  • Komodo: Apr-Oct for dragon sightings and diving. Nov-Mar rougher seas.
  • Raja Ampat: Oct-Apr best for diving visibility. May-Sep windier.

Festivals to Plan Around

  • Nyepi (March): Bali's Day of Silence. Airport closed. Unique but limiting.
  • Waisak (May): Buddha's birthday — spectacular ceremony at Borobudur.
  • Ramadan & Lebaran: Dates shift yearly. Many restaurants closed during day, but vibrant night markets.
  • Galungan: Every 210 days. Bali's most important Hindu festival.
  • Indonesia Independence Day (Aug 17): Celebrations nationwide.

Pro Tip

If you're flexible, visit in May or September. The weather is excellent, flights are cheaper, and you'll avoid the July-August European summer rush.
