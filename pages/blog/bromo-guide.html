Mount Bromo Guide

Sunrise trek, costs, and everything you need to know

Destination Guide • 8 min read

Mount Bromo is Indonesia's most photographed volcano — and for good reason. The view of its smoldering crater surrounded by a sea of volcanic sand, with Mount Semeru towering behind, is absolutely surreal.

Mount Bromo Tour

$30-60 per person

Includes jeep transport, sunrise viewpoint, crater hike, and hotel pickup

How to Get to Bromo

From Surabaya (closest city)

  • Bus: Surabaya to Probolinggo $5 (2h), then jeep to Bromo $10-15 (1h)
  • Tour package: 2D/1N from Surabaya $50-80 per person
  • Private car: $60-80 for entire group (4-6 people)

From Yogyakarta

  • Train + tour: Yogya to Surabaya train $15-25 (4h), then join Bromo tour from Surabaya
  • Tour package: 3D/2N Yogya-Bromo-Ijen $120-180 (most popular option)
  • Private driver: $100-150 for direct transfer

Trek Details

DetailInformation
DifficultyEasy-Moderate (stairs to crater rim)
Duration45min-1h to crater rim
Start time3:00-3:30am for sunrise
Elevation2,329m (7,641ft)
Temperature5-15°C at summit (bring a jacket!)
Entry fee$25 (included in most tours)

What to Expect (Timeline)

  • 1:00am-2:00am — Pick up from hotel in jeep
  • 3:30am — Arrive at Penanjakan viewpoint (watch sunrise at 5:00-5:30am)
  • 5:30am — Descend to the Sea of Sand
  • 6:00am — Walk to crater rim (45min) or take a horse ($10)
  • 7:00am — Explore the crater (stay on the rim, fumes are toxic)
  • 8:00am — Return to jeep, drive back to hotel
  • 9:00am-10:00am — Breakfast and rest

Packing List

  • Warm jacket — essential, it's freezing before sunrise
  • Head torch — for the early morning hike
  • Face mask — the volcanic sand gets everywhere
  • Closed shoes — the crater path is rocky and dusty
  • Water & snacks — available at viewpoint but expensive
  • Camera — sunrise over Bromo is the money shot

Pro Tips

Book a tour that goes to Penanjakan viewpoint (viewpoint #1) — most iconic shot. The trek to the crater rim is optional but highly recommended. Go on a weekday to avoid the worst crowds. Check the live webcam before booking — cloudy mornings are disappointing.

Best Bromo + Ijen Combo Tour
