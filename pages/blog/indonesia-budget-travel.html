Indonesia on $40/Day

The complete budget backpacker's guide

Budget Guide • 10 min read

Indonesia is one of Southeast Asia's cheapest travel destinations. With $40-50 per day you can live well — eat great food, stay in comfortable accommodation, and see incredible sights. Here's exactly how to budget.

Average Daily Costs

Where Your Money Goes

Accommodation

  • Hostel dorm: $8-12/night (Bali, Yogyakarta)
  • Budget guesthouse: $12-20/night (private room, fan)
  • Mid-range hotel: $25-50/night (AC, breakfast, pool)
  • Homestay: $10-15/night (includes meals in remote areas)

Food

  • Nasi Goreng/Warung meal: $2-4
  • Restaurant dinner: $5-10
  • Street food snack: $0.50-1
  • Bottle of water: $0.30-0.50
  • Bintang beer: $2-3

Transport

  • Scooter rental: $5-8/day
  • Go-Jek (short ride): $1-3
  • Train (Yogya-Surabaya): $8-15
  • Ferry (Bali-Lombok): $15-25
  • Domestic flight: $30-80

Sample Weekly Budget ($40/day)

  • Day 1-3 (Bali): Hostel $36 + food $21 + scooter $18 + activities $15 = $90
  • Day 4 (Travel): Ferry to Lombok $20 + lunch $4 + hostel $12 = $36
  • Day 5-7 (Lombok/Gili): Guesthouse $51 + food $24 + bike rental $15 + snorkel $10 = $100
  • Total 7 days: ~$280 (not including flights to/from Indonesia)

#1 Budget Tip

Eat at warungs (local food stalls). They serve authentic Indonesian food for $2-4 per meal — and it's often better than restaurant food. Look for the ones packed with locals.

Cheapest Destinations

  • Yogyakarta: $25-35/day. Most affordable city in Indonesia.
  • Sumatra: $25-40/day. Bukit Lawang, Lake Toba, Padang.
  • Bali (off-peak): $35-50/day. Canggu, Ubud, Sanur.
  • Lombok: $30-45/day. Quieter, cheaper alternative to Bali.
  • Sulawesi: $25-35/day. Tana Toraja, Bunaken diving.

ATM & Money Tips

  • ATMs charge $3-5 per withdrawal. Withdraw large amounts at once.
  • Bring a Wise or Revolut card for best exchange rates.
  • Carry cash for small shops, warungs, and remote areas.
  • Credit cards accepted at hotels and tourist restaurants (3% surcharge common).
  • Exchange rates at money changers in tourist areas are competitive.
