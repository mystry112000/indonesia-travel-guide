3-Day Bali Itinerary

Bali on a budget — temples, rice terraces, and volcanoes

Itinerary • 6 min read

Bali is compact enough to experience its highlights in just 3 days. This itinerary covers the best of Bali's culture, nature, and beaches without rushing — all for under $150 total including transport, food, and entry fees.

Total Cost: $130-160 per person

Accommodation: $45-75 (3 nights) • Food: $25-40 • Transport: $30-45 • Entry fees: $20-30

Getting Around

Rent a scooter ($5-8/day) for flexibility. Otherwise, use Go-Jek for short rides or hire a private driver ($35-45/day) for day trips.

Day 1 — Ubud: Temples & Rice Terraces

  1. 6:30am — Visit Tegalalang Rice Terraces before crowds (free walk).
  2. 9:00am — Explore Tirta Empul holy water temple.
  3. 11:00am — Ubud Monkey Forest ($5 entry).
  4. 12:30pm — Lunch at Warung Babi Guling Ibu Oka ($3-5).
  5. 2:00pm — Ubud Art Market & Palace (free).
  6. 5:00pm — Sunset at Campuhan Ridge Walk (free).

Day 2 — Uluwatu & South Coast

  1. 8:00am — Drive to Uluwatu (1.5h from Ubud).
  2. 10:00am — Visit Uluwatu Temple ($5, sarong rental $1).
  3. 12:00pm — Lunch at Single Fin cliffside ($8-12).
  4. 2:00pm — Chill at Padang Padang or Bingin Beach (free).
  5. 5:30pm — Watch Kecak Fire Dance at Uluwatu ($10).
  6. 7:30pm — Dinner at Jimbaran Bay seafood ($10-15).

Day 3 — Mount Batur Sunrise & Seminyak

  1. 1:30am — Pick up for Mount Batur sunrise trek ($35-45, includes guide & breakfast).
  2. 6:00am — Summit sunrise (1.5h hike, moderate difficulty).
  3. 9:00am — Return, breakfast, rest.
  4. 2:00pm — Seminyak beach afternoon & shopping.
  5. 5:00pm — Sunset at La Plancha beanbags (drinks $3-5).

Money-Saving Tips

Eat at local warungs instead of tourist restaurants ($3 vs $10 meals). Book tours directly in Ubud (cheaper than online). Use Go-Jek instead of taxis for short trips. ATM fees are high — withdraw large amounts at once.
