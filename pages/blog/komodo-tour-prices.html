Komodo Tour Prices

Complete 2025 cost guide for Komodo National Park

Budget Guide • 7 min read

Komodo National Park is one of Indonesia's most expensive destinations, but with the right planning you can visit without breaking the bank. Here's every cost broken down.

Komodo Entry Fee (2025)

$25

Conservation + park entry. Pay at Labuan Bajo or ranger stations.

Additional Costs

  • Flight to Labuan Bajo (LBJ): From Bali $80-150 return
  • Accommodation: Hostel $10-15, mid-range $30-50, luxury $80-150/night
  • Snorkel gear rental: $5-10 per day
  • Ranger fee: Included in tour (required for dragon trekking)
  • Tips: Not mandatory but appreciated ($5-10/day for guides)

Sample Budget

  • 3-day trip (economy): Flight $120 + hostel $45 + budget tour $130 + food $40 = $335 total
  • 4-day trip (mid-range): Flight $120 + hotel $160 + liveaboard $250 + food $60 = $590 total
  • 5-day trip (luxury): Flight $150 + resort $400 + luxury liveaboard $700 + dining $100 = $1,350 total

Money-Saving Tips

  • Book in groups: Shared day trips drop to $50/person with 4+ people
  • Travel shoulder season: March-June and September-October for lower prices
  • Pack snorkel gear: Saves $5-10/day rental
  • Avoid July-August: Peak season prices are 30-50% higher
  • Eat local: Warungs in Labuan Bajo serve great food for $3-5
