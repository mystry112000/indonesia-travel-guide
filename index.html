Why Indonesia?

Unmatched Diversity

Nowhere else on Earth packs so much variety into one country. Volcanoes, rainforests, coral reefs, ancient temples, and hundreds of unique cultures — all in one archipelago.

  • Cheapest travel in Southeast Asia — $30-50/day budget
  • Year-round destination — dry season Apr-Oct
  • Visa-free for 169 countries (30 days)
  • English widely spoken in tourist areas
  • Incredible food — #1 cuisine in the world (CNN)
